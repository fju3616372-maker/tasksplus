Everyday Uses & Purpose

01  Personal Daily Planning & Habit Routines
Organize one-time errands and repeating Daily, Weekly, Custom-Day, Monthly, or Annual routines with optional color-coded categories, urgency thermometers, and a collapsible Upcoming Recurring drawer that keeps today's list clutter-free.
02  Time-Boxed Focus Sprints
Pair selected tasks with a live countdown timer accessible inside Settings, persistent notifications, and a home-screen widget—backed by a guaranteed high-priority Time's Up alert when the sprint finishes.
03  Family & Team Accountability
Build shared Family or Organization groups with Manager, Parent/Admin, Member, and Child roles. Assign tasks, attach optional photo proof, and verify completions automatically with Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) or manual review.
04  Mindful Screen-Time & Positive Motivation
Set hourly, daily, or weekly per-app usage limits and task-gated app locks, reward early completions with customizable Credit Points, unlock profile emblems, and compare progress on the opt-in Global Leaderboard.

Features & Core Functions

Designed offline-first so every action is instant on device, while syncing seamlessly across Android devices via Google Sign-In and Firestore.

01 • CATEGORIES & MOTION
Color Labels & Ripple Checkmarks
Toggle optional categories with 6 premade presets (Work, Personal, Shopping, Health, Study, Home) plus up to 30 custom labels in 10 colors. Includes a slim top filter bar, card badges, instant search, and a radial ripple + spring-popped checkmark animation on completion.
02 • URGENCY & ALERTS
1–5 Urgency Thermometer & Alarms
Glide edge-to-edge across a custom green-to-red thermometer from Calm (1) to Critical (5). Pair tasks with configurable interval reminders via AlarmManager, optional wake-up alarm bells, and guided permission checks for exact alarms and battery guard.
03 • FOCUS TIMER
Task Countdown Timer & Time's Up Alert
Assign one or more tasks to a timed focus session with customizable default durations, pause/resume controls, live notification countdowns, a dedicated home-screen Timer Widget, and a guaranteed high-priority Time's Up alert when time expires.
04 • INSIGHTS
Daily Productivity Bar Chart
Track Completed vs. Pending tasks across Today or the past 7 Days in a custom grouped bar chart. Toggle the Productivity Dashboard inside the To-Do view or pin it directly to your Android home screen as a widget.
05 • GROUPS & AI PROOF
Shared Groups, Gemini & App Locks
Invite members by email, assign group tasks, verify photo proofs via Gemini AI auto-approval, monitor real-time app usage via UsageStatsManager, and enforce per-app screen-time limits or task-to-unlock rewards.
06 • MOTIVATION & THEMES
Credits, Leaderboard & M3 Themes
Customize early-completion rewards, grace periods, and late penalties with an immutable credit ledger, unlock profile badges, join the opt-in Global Leaderboard, personalize with Dynamic Color + 5 palettes, and update directly via GitHub Releases.

Built the Jetpack Compose M3 interface, custom adaptive launcher icon, Room SQLite database, recurring schedule calculator, AlarmManager reminder pipeline, dynamic theming with 5 tonal palettes, and Android home-screen widgets.

Engineering Notes & Lessons

Behind the clean Material 3 surfaces sit several tough Android & cloud engineering problems solved along the way.

01  Solving Recurring Task Duplication in Cloud Sync
Kotlin's isCompleted boolean property naming caused Firestore reflection to miss completed states on recurring routines, spawning duplicate copies. Switching to explicit snapshot parsing, @PropertyName parity, atomic Room + Cloud writes, and a dedicated Upcoming Recurring drawer made daily habits rock-solid.
02  Guaranteed Time's Up Notifications Under OS Limits
Android background throttling can delay ordinary timers. Combining exact AlarmManager expiration schedules, synchronous receiver execution, and a dedicated high-priority alert channel ensures Time's Up alerts fire reliably even when the screen is off.
03  Custom Visual Craft Across Android 6.0 to Android 16
Maintaining minSdk = 23 while targeting API 36 meant building custom Canvas components—the edge-to-edge Urgency Thermometer, Productivity Bar Chart, and Ripple Checkmark—without relying on modern-only APIs, verified by automated Marshmallow Robolectric tests.
04  Zero-Trust Security Parity on Every Feature
Every schema update—including Group role permissions, Global Leaderboard records, and Task Categories—was validated end-to-end across Room migrations, Firestore rules, and a 9-case local emulator test suite before deployment.
